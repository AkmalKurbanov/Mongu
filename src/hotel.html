Comfort Meets Adventure

We know exactly what it means to return from Lenin Peak or after a week-long off-road expedition. Mongu Hotel was built by travelers, specifically for travelers.

Expect more than just a spotless bed. We offer specialized infrastructure: from dedicated gear drying rooms to an alpine breakfast served early for those hitting the trail at dawn.

Designed for Explorers

  • Free Wi-Fi

    High-speed internet access available throughout the entire property.

  • Ski & Mountain Gear Rental

    Professional rental service for skis, snowboards, and high-altitude trekking equipment right on the premises.

  • Free Parking

    Secure, gated parking area for private and overland vehicles.

  • Airport Shuttle

    Direct transfer services to and from the airport on request.

  • Non-Smoking Rooms

    Clean, fresh, and smoke-free environment across all rooms.

  • Room Service

    Prompt food and beverage delivery directly to your door.

  • Delicious Breakfast

    Hearty and fresh morning meals available daily at the hotel.

  • Gear Drying Room

    Professional heated facility to dry your boots and outdoor clothing.
